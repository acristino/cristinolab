01 — About

Where genomics meets neuroscience

The Neurogenomics and Systems Biology Laboratory is focused on understanding how genetic variation and regulatory non-coding elements contribute to the aetiology of complex neuropsychiatric disorders, with a particular emphasis on schizophrenia and neurodevelopmental conditions.

We develop computational pipelines and algorithms to interrogate large-scale genomic and transcriptomic datasets — integrating GWAS findings, eQTL analyses, regulatory element annotation (cCREs, JASPAR), and small RNA biology (miRNA, circRNA) to build mechanistic models of gene dysregulation in disease.

Our work bridges wet-lab molecular biology and high-performance computation, enabling discovery across scales from individual variants to systems-level networks.

02 — Research

Our research areas

Schizophrenia Genetics

Regulatory Genomics of Neuropsychiatric Disease

Integrating GWAS, cCRE annotation, and transcription factor binding data to resolve the functional impact of risk variants at the MIR137/MIR2682 locus and beyond.

Small RNA Biology

miRNA & circRNA Networks

Characterising the regulatory roles of microRNAs and circular RNAs in neuronal development and disease — from target prediction pipelines to functional validation in model systems.

Systems Biology

PPI Networks & Pathway Modelling

Building protein–protein interaction networks from BioGRID and applying permutation-based statistics to identify convergent disease pathways across genetic datasets.

Comparative Genomics

Evolutionary Constraint & Recombination

Leveraging multi-species alignments and phylogenetic approaches (HyPhy GARD, IQ-TREE2) to identify conserved functional elements under selection in the human genome.

Computational Neuroscience

Cortical Circuit Modelling

Developing biologically-inspired laminar cortical network models with GPU acceleration to simulate population-level dynamics relevant to neuropsychiatric phenotypes.

Pipeline Development

Bioinformatics Tooling

Engineering scalable, reproducible analysis pipelines for HPC environments — from FASTA preprocessing and parquet-based data warehousing to GPU-accelerated enrichment analyses.
