Get in touch

We welcome inquiries from prospective students, postdoctoral researchers, and collaborative partners. Please reach out if you are interested in joining the lab or discussing research opportunities.

We are always interested in hearing from talented and motivated individuals at any career stage — from Honours and PhD candidates to experienced postdoctoral researchers and visiting scientists.

For collaboration inquiries, please include a brief description of your research and how it might connect with our programmes. For student and postdoc applications, please attach a CV and brief statement of research interests.

Opportunities

PhD Candidates

PhD Scholarships

We seek motivated PhD candidates with backgrounds in molecular biology, bioinformatics, genomics, or computational biology. Griffith University offers a range of domestic scholarship opportunities.

Postdoctoral

Postdoctoral Positions

Postdoctoral positions are available on funded projects when vacancies arise. We also support applications for fellowship schemes (NHMRC Early Career Fellowship, ARC DECRA) by strong candidates.

Collaboration

Research Collaborations

We welcome discussions with academic and industry partners on collaborative research projects. Relevant areas include neurogenomics, miRNA biology, cancer immunotherapy, and bioinformatics tool development.
